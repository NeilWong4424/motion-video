# Premium quality contract

This contract establishes the normative quality bar for every film: the standard of a top-tier international
2D motion-graphics house — Apple-Keynote restraint crossed with broadcast-title gravitas. It is the home of
"director-grade" and "premium," which no other contract encodes. Part 1 implements no validator; this is a
documentation contract that the `creative-direction` role authors *to* and the `creative-reviewer` role grades
*against*. It never weakens `continuity-first`, truth, rights, or scope; it raises the aesthetic ceiling above
the comprehension floor those rules guarantee.

## The premium bar (definition of done)

A film meets the bar when a neutral viewer, shown it beside a real broadcast title package, cannot immediately
dismiss it as amateur. Concretely, every beat must satisfy all four:

1. **A single composed focal hero** — one clear thing the eye is led to; everything else supports it.
2. **Eased motion with follow-through** — no `linear`/`hold`-snapped hero motion; entrances ease and settle,
   heroes may overshoot subtly (`easeOutBack`). Nothing appears or vanishes without a motivated enter/exit.
3. **At least one layer of depth** — a soft shadow, a subtle 2–3-stop gradient, or shaded faces; never a flat
   crayon fill on the hero.
4. **A seamless bridge in and out** — a shared-element, camera-navigation, morph, match-on-action, or
   directional-push handoff; never a hard slide or full-frame replacement.

And across the whole film:

- **One promise, delivered** — an emotional promise set in the opening and paid off in the close.
- **Restraint** — one accent color system (default Broadcast Noir), large negative space, exactly one
  glow/bloom reserved for the single hero payoff, no rainbow/high-chroma psychedelia.
- **Typographic discipline** — the real size ladder, tight display tracking, two-tone accent for emphasis,
  per-glyph kinetic entrances that resolve rather than wobble.
- **Finish** — consistent radii, aligned optical spacing, clean vector edges, no orphaned elements.

## Authoring obligation (creative-direction)

The Treatment must declare an `emotionalArc` (the felt journey), a per-beat `audienceTakeaway` (what the viewer
feels and the unspoken line), and a `premiumTarget` (the reference bar). It must build the smallest arc that
still MOVES someone — not the smallest arc as flat information states. It must load the `storytelling`,
`emotional-pacing`, `shot-language`, and `premium-taste` craft skills before authoring.

## Review obligation (creative-reviewer)

The creative review must grade the film against this bar in addition to the comprehension floor. A film that is
merely clear, on-message, and not-a-slideshow but that fails the premium bar is a quality defect: the reviewer
records it (typically a `fix` disposition) rather than shipping it. The reviewer grades each beat against the
four per-beat criteria above and the film against the whole-film criteria, checking the Treatment's declared
`audienceTakeaway` is actually delivered on screen.

## Boundaries

This contract adds no authority to write, approve, render, or bypass a gate. It does not permit inventing
facts, rights, or CTAs to manufacture drama; premium quality comes from the ordering, emphasis, motion, and
finish of verified material only. It never overrides truth, rights, continuity law, scope, or the review
precedence `rebuild > fix > ship`.
