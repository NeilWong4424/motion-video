# Shot Language

## Purpose / Use when

Use when composing a beat's frame as *expression* — where to place the focal hero, how much to withhold, how
the eye is led. This complements `camera-choreography.md` (which governs truthful spatial navigation) by
addressing composition as a storytelling and premium-craft device.

## Reads

Read the Treatment's beat intentions and focal hierarchy, `camera-choreography.md`, `style-system.md`,
`design-system.md`, and `premium-taste.md`. Use the role-owned context supplied by the loader.

## Writes

None. This module performs no artifact writes.

## Must

- **Direct the eye like a conductor's baton.** Camera, contrast, motion, and the single accent color conspire
  to move one focal point through the frame. Never let the viewer wonder where to look; continuity of gaze is
  the seamless-motion gate honored as composition.
- **Use negative space as a design element.** A confident frame is often 90% empty with one line of type or one
  hero mark. Scale for awe: make the thing that matters physically large in the frame when the beat's job is
  scale.
- **Compose one focal hero per beat.** Everything else supports it — quieter, smaller, lower contrast. Give the
  hero optical weight (position, size, isolation), not just center placement.
- **Withhold deliberately.** Reveal the payoff last and alone. Restraint — knowing what to leave out — is the
  premium move; over-decoration reads cheap.
- **Establish a single, consistent light.** One light direction across shadows and shaded faces (e.g. iso
  stacks) so the world feels coherent and three-dimensional without real 3D.
- **Keep the frame finished.** Consistent radii, aligned optical spacing, clean vector edges, deliberate enter
  AND exit for every element (nothing simply vanishes or pops without reason).

## Must not

- Crowd the frame with competing focal points, decorative clutter, or tiny anchor elements.
- Center everything by default; reach for balance and tension, not just symmetry.
- Let an element appear or disappear without a motivated enter/exit.
- Contradict `camera-choreography.md`'s truth rules to chase a composition (no fake spatiality).

## Stop conditions

Stop when a composition needs a spatial relationship the facts do not support, hides unclear copy, or fights
the beat's declared focal hierarchy.

## Output schema

None.
