# Continuous World

## Purpose / Use when

Use when an overview-to-detail, nesting, adjacency, scale, or route is genuinely spatial and can be explained by one mounted world and a global camera.

## Reads

Read the Treatment composition mode, `continuity-first.md`, `camera-choreography.md`, and verified spatial facts or supplied assets.

## Writes

None. This module performs no artifact writes.

## Must

- Keep the Persistent World mounted once and preserve stable identity and shared coordinates through the travel.
- Name the actual relationship a camera travel reveals: for example containment, proximity, sequence, or a meaningful scale change.
- Make travel eye-trackable with one primary verb by default and a traceable bridge; use a global camera rather than local scene cameras. Combine verbs only when a semantic relationship requires the combination, and record the rationale plus the continuous eye trace.
- Prefer a held camera with live content when travel adds no understanding.
- Use the real target and preroll it when a target must take over a bridge.

## Must not

- Force continuous-world staging when persistent-stage, held-shot, shared-element, or match-on-action is more truthful.
- Teleport camera state, fabricate geography, or move permanently without narrative reason.

## Stop conditions

Stop when no real spatial relationship exists, a travel obscures the focal node or eye trace, or a target cannot remain a stable real target.

## Output schema

None.
