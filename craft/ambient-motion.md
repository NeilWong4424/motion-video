# Ambient Motion

## Purpose / Use when

Use for subordinate atmosphere such as low-contrast texture, light, field movement, or background response that supports a focal action.

## Reads

Read the Treatment, `style-system.md`, `motion-craft.md`, and the focal-content plan.

## Writes

None. This module performs no artifact writes.

## Must

- Keep ambient motion lower in contrast, amplitude, and attention than the focal layer.
- Tie atmosphere to the established visual system and use it to maintain a living held shot, not to manufacture urgency.
- Keep motion deterministic and profile-bound; ranges are starting ranges, never universal rules.
- Reduce or hold ambient activity when it competes with reading, data interpretation, UI actions, or logo resolve.

## Must not

- Use ambient movement as permanent movement, an excuse for a dead frame, or a replacement for a bridge.
- Introduce unsupported imagery, visual noise, flicker that impairs accessibility, or an unrelated style language.

## Stop conditions

Stop when ambient motion competes with the eye trace, compromises readability, creates motion sickness, or contradicts the approved system.

## Output schema

None.
