# General Motion Craft

## Purpose / Use when

Use for a motion-language decision that crosses domains: hierarchy, staging, holds, easing, focal shifts, and transition vocabulary.

## Reads

Read the approved Brief and Treatment, motion profile, `continuity-first.md`, and the selected domain module.

## Writes

None. This module performs no artifact writes.

## Must

- Make every visible movement serve arrival, emphasis, explanation, or a motivated change of state.
- Keep one primary focal node per Beat and preserve a readable hold after consequential information settles.
- Treat a Beat as a narrative state, not a slide; evolve the Persistent World through a declared bridge.
- Use a small, coherent vocabulary of easing and motion families. Starting ranges belong to the approved motion profile and are never universal timing rules.
- Prefer a held camera and live internal content over movement that merely fills time.
- Let hierarchy decide amplitude and speed: background is quieter, supporting elements follow, and the focal action leads.

## Must not

- Treat constant movement as quality, turn every change into a flourish, or make timing ranges universal rules.
- Use motion to hide unclear copy, unsupported facts, or a missing continuity relationship.
- Add a transition family outside the Treatment vocabulary.

## Stop conditions

Stop when a proposed movement contradicts the message, accessibility, readability, motion profile, or continuity policy. Route a missing capability through `../docs/workflows/capability-gap.md`.

## Output schema

None.
