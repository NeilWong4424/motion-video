# Continuity First

## Purpose / Use when

Use for every Beat boundary and any decision involving persistent identity, a bridge, a cut, a handoff, or a content change. This is the normative craft expression of `seamless-default`.

## Reads

Read the approved Treatment, MotionSpec continuity intent, adjacent Beat identities, continuity policy, and current review evidence. This module is self-contained and has no craft dependency.

## Writes

None. This module performs no artifact writes.

## Must

- State the rule plainly: a Beat is a narrative state, not a slide.
- Preserve one global clock, a Persistent World mounted for the full film, stable identity for a persisting node, and one global camera.
- Declare one bridge for every adjacent Beat pair. Prefer, in order: same-node shared-element transformation; camera navigation revealing a real spatial relationship; morph into the real target; match on action; directional push; then a justified chapter cut.
- Bind every bridge through its exact `treatmentCameraRationaleId`. A `camera-navigation` bridge consumes the same-position `travel` rationale and preserves its `revealedSpatialRelation`; every non-camera bridge consumes the same-position `hold` rationale and cannot introduce boundary camera travel.
- Keep the target preroll mounted early and frozen when a split handoff is unavoidable; it must be the stable real target, never a drawn substitute.
- Prohibit a dead frame: no bare, transparent, accidental black, or unmotivated empty frame during a handoff.
- Require a declared content transition for changing text or content inside a bridge. For an exact handoff, terminal geometry equals the next stable state within the declared tolerance.
- For every positive-duration bridge, document before, midpoint, and after frames, including eye trace, target preroll where applicable, terminal geometry, and endpoint stability. Review playback at 1.0× and 0.25×.
- For the zero-frame chapter cut, do not invent a midpoint. Require the outgoing Beat's last frame, the incoming Beat's first frame, the incoming first held frame, outgoing and incoming eye-trace positions, and full-frame-change evidence.
- Permit a maximum of one chapter cut in the whole film, only with an honest semantic, temporal, spatial, or emotional break; a Treatment budget; zero duration; outgoing and incoming eye-trace anchors; written justification; and no adjacent chapter cut.

## Must not

- Let style weaken continuity, use repeated page replacement, fake a morph or spatial relationship, or use a decorative speck as a continuity excuse.
- Equate seamless with permanent movement.
- Use a chapter cut to repair an ordinary planning problem or exceed the maximum one chapter cut.

## Stop conditions

Stop when no honest bridge exists and the chapter-cut exception is unavailable; when a node declared as persisting across a non-cut bridge cannot retain stable identity; when the evidence required for the declared bridge mode is absent; or when slide-like rhythm persists. Do not reject an honest chapter cut merely because no persistent identity is claimed across it.

## Output schema

None.
