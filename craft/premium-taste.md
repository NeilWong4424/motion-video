# Premium Taste

## Purpose / Use when

Use on every Treatment and every creative review as the reference bar: the standard of a top-tier international
2D motion-graphics house — Apple-Keynote restraint crossed with broadcast-title gravitas. This module is the
"50-year master director" voice, written as checkable principles, and it is the home of the premium
definition-of-done that the mechanical craft modules lack.

## Reads

Read the Brief, the Treatment (or the resolved film under review), `design-system.md`, `style-system.md`,
`storytelling.md`, `shot-language.md`, and `emotional-pacing.md`, plus the premium-quality contract. Use the
role-owned context supplied by the loader.

## Writes

None. This module performs no artifact writes. Creative-direction authors to this bar; creative-reviewer grades
against it.

## Must

- **Restraint over spectacle.** One clear idea per beat; move one thing; glow exactly once (reserve bloom for
  the single hero payoff). Luxury is what you withhold.
- **Motion has weight and follow-through.** Eased entrances, subtle overshoot (`easeOutBack`) on hero elements,
  a secondary settle. Objects imply mass. Nothing linear, nothing hold-snapped, unless a hard cut is the
  intent.
- **Considered, disciplined color.** Anchor on a coherent pack (default: Broadcast Noir — deep near-black
  field, ONE electric accent, large negative space). Gradients are subtle (2–3 stops, low chroma spread), never
  rainbow noise or high-saturation clashing.
- **Typographic discipline.** Use the real size ladder (display / headline / title / body / caption), tight
  tracking on display type, two-tone accent for emphasis, per-glyph kinetic entrances that *resolve* (settle),
  never wobble.
- **Depth and light.** Layered soft shadows, one consistent light direction, one restrained glow/bloom on the
  hero moment only.
- **Finish.** Consistent corner radii, aligned optical spacing, sub-pixel-clean edges, no orphaned elements, a
  deliberate enter and exit for every node.

## Definition of done (the premium bar)

A neutral viewer, shown this beside a real broadcast title package, cannot immediately dismiss it as amateur.
Each beat has: a clear focal hero, eased motion with follow-through, at least one layer of depth
(shadow/gradient), and a seamless bridge in and out. Reviewed scene-by-scene against this bar before sign-off.

## Must not

- Ship crayon primaries, default system type, eyeballed layout, or rainbow/psychedelic high-chroma fields.
- Over-decorate, drift for its own sake, or glow more than the single hero moment.
- Confuse "coherent and on-message" (a floor) with "premium" (the ceiling this module sets).
- Let a mechanical pass (Technical QC green, schema valid) substitute for the premium bar.

## Stop conditions

Stop and flag when the plan or film meets the comprehension floor but fails the premium bar — that is a
quality defect to raise, not to wave through.
