# Logo Motion

## Purpose / Use when

Use for a supplied, licensed logo or identity asset that needs a restrained introduction, resolve, or end-card role.

## Reads

Read local asset provenance and license status, brand constraints, the Treatment, and `style-system.md`.

## Writes

None. This module performs no artifact writes.

## Must

- Preserve the approved mark's proportions, clear space, colors, and recognizable geometry.
- Use a restrained reveal or resolve motivated by the narrative, then let the final mark settle and stay still.
- Keep the mark as a stable real target when it spans a bridge; validate contrast and legibility at delivery size.
- Use profile-bound starting ranges only and prioritize recognition over spectacle.

## Must not

- Invent, redraw, warp, fragment, or recolor a protected mark without authorization.
- Use remote or unlicensed files, make the logo a perpetual motion device, or let brand treatment override continuity.

## Stop conditions

Stop when provenance or permission is unclear, brand constraints conflict with the Treatment, or the final mark cannot remain legible and stable.

## Output schema

None.
