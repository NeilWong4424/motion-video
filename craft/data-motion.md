# Data Motion

## Purpose / Use when

Use for charts, quantified comparisons, value changes, and data-backed visual explanations.

## Reads

Read verified Brief facts, source labels and units, approved Treatment, and `motion-craft.md`. This module expresses any value-bearing relationship from those inputs without reverse-loading the diagram skill.

## Writes

None. This module performs no artifact writes.

## Must

- Preserve verified values, ordering, units, uncertainty, and scope; declare any interpolation as visual transition rather than new data.
- Reveal one comparison or relationship at a time, then hold settled values long enough to read.
- Keep chart marks, labels, axes, and diagram anchors stable when their identity persists.
- Make motion explain order, grouping, change, or comparison without changing perceived magnitude.
- Use profile-bound starting ranges only and prioritize legibility over tempo.

## Must not

- Invent numbers, imply unsupported causality, alter scale for drama, or conceal a denominator, unit, or qualifier.
- Rebuild the whole chart as a slide when a stable mark or anchor can bridge the change.

## Stop conditions

Stop when data is unverified, labels cannot remain legible, scale or motion would misrepresent magnitude/order, or a requested claim exceeds the source.

## Output schema

None.
